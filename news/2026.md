### Supplementary Note on the January 1, 2026 Report Concerning π and Approximately 67%

With regard to the report published on January 1, 2026, “Verification Results of the Circulation Structure: The Social Circulation Rate Corresponding to π Is Approximately 67%,” this note provides a supplementary clarification from the perspective of mathematical refinement.

In the January 1 report, we presented the fact that both the recovery rate and the reserve ratio converged at approximately 67%, and raised the question of how this observation relates to π as a structural indicator.

However, at present, no rigorous mathematical derivation establishes a direct functional relationship between the approximately 67% operational observation and π as a structural equilibrium indicator.

The expression

R ≈ f(π)

presented in the January 1 article was not intended to represent a mathematically defined functional relationship, but rather a symbolic expression indicating a conceptual correspondence based on structural observation.

Accordingly, the facts that can be confirmed as of today are as follows:
・It is an empirically observed fact that both the recovery rate and the reserve ratio coincided at approximately 67%.
・π is a theoretical indicator representing the structural state in which circulation is established.
・Whether a direct mathematical mapping exists between the two remains unverified.

Therefore, we do not assert that the value of approximately 67% is derived from π.

In this system, the approximately 67% figure represents an operational stability level observed as of January 2026.
How this level relates to structural equilibrium (π) remains a subject for future investigation.

The 67% / 33% distinction was presented as an observational categorization of system connectivity and was not intended to be established as a mathematical law.

The approximately 67% figure coincided with a previously set social participation target (such as voter turnout) within practical activities.
However, this numerical coincidence should not be interpreted as evidence of structural causation, but rather as an observational parallel at this stage.

The system remains in a small-scale experimental phase.
Going forward, we will continue verification while clearly distinguishing between inference and formal proof in examining the relationship between empirical data and the theoretical structure.

This concludes the technical clarification regarding the report dated January 1.

### Verification Results of the Circulation Structure:
### The Social Circulation Rate Corresponding to π Is Approximately 67%

This system is an actually existing institution accompanied by implementation and operation, and this paper presents an analysis based on empirical verification.

In the operation of this system, both the recovery rate and the reserve (allocation) rate were observed to be approximately 67%.

This value was not intentionally set at the design stage of the system; rather, it emerged as an observed result of real-world operation.

This correspondence can be expressed mathematically as follows:

𝑅≈𝑓(𝜋)≈0.67

Here, R represents the proportion that actually returned to circulation within the system (i.e., the recovery rate and the reserve rate).
The function f represents the relationship through which the state in which circulation is established (π), via the recorded actions of people connected to the system, manifests as a concrete numerical value in reality.

At present, it is unclear whether this approximate value of 67% represents a fixed limiting value.
However, the fact that two different indicators—the recovery rate and the reserve rate—coincide at the same value provides an important insight into the nature of circulation within this structure.

The verification results of this system therefore raise the following question:

“Does π manifest itself as approximately 67%?”

What follows presents the perspective of the implementer, taking this premise as a starting point.

67% → People who are currently connectable
33% → People who are currently not connectable

No matter how well-designed a system may be, only about 67% of people can be connected simultaneously.
The remaining 33% require factors on the individual side—such as time, personality, or values—rather than the system itself.

It is unclear whether 67% should be considered a large or small number, but from the implementer’s perspective, it honestly feels smaller than expected.

Drawing a boundary with the remaining 33% of people requires courage and demands judgment based on structure rather than emotion.

If one chooses this structure and continues actions grounded in goodwill within reasonable limits, it should be possible to reduce experiences of poverty, loneliness, and despair.
However, the verification of this system also demonstrates a reality: when participation remains at the level of one-sided receipt, even if there is prayer and action based upon it, circulation does not necessarily continue—in other words, it does not always connect to the future.

That said, this outcome is neither good nor bad in itself; rather, it serves as one criterion by which the sustainability of the system can be evaluated.

This analysis is based on verification results as of January 2026, and it is possible that this number may change as humanity itself evolves.

Separately from this structure, a target of approximately 67% was also set in social practice as a progressive and sustainable rate of social participation (voter turnout).
The fact that these two values coincided as outcomes is noteworthy.
This corresponds to roughly two-thirds of the whole.

Within this structure, π is a structural indicator representing the state in which circulation is established.
In human society, the value of approximately 67% was observed as one numerical point at which social stability and harmony are simultaneously achieved, and this is the conclusion drawn from the verification.

Note:
The 67% / 33% figures in this paper indicate the state of connection to the system.
They do not include people for whom contribution itself has become difficult and who have therefore stepped outside the system.

### 循環構造の検証結果：πに対応する社会循環率は約67%

本制度は、実装と運用を伴う実在の制度であり、本稿はその検証に基づく分析である。

本制度の運用において、回収率および引当率はいずれも 約67% という値を示した。

これは、制度設計の段階で意図した数値ではなく、実際の運用結果として観測されたものである。

この一致を数式的に表すと、次のように書ける。

𝑅≈𝑓(𝜋)≈0.67

ここで R は、制度運用の中で実際に循環へ戻ってきた割合（回収率および引当率）を表す。
また f は、制度に接続された人々の行動記録を通じて、循環が成立している状態（π）が、現実の数値としてどのように現れたかを表す関係である。

現時点では、この約67%という値が固定された極限であるかどうかは不明である。
しかし、回収率と引当率という異なる指標が、同じ値で一致しているという事実は、本構造における循環のあり方を考える上で重要な示唆を与える。

本制度の検証結果は、「π は約67%として現れるのではないか」という問いを提示するものである。

以下は、それを前提に実装者としての見解を示すものである。

67% → 今、接続可能な人
33% → 今は接続不能な人

どんなに整った制度でも、同時に接続できるのは約67%までであり、残り33%は、制度ではなく時間や性格、価値観といった個人側の要因を必要とする。

67%という数字は多いのか少ないのかわからないが実装者の感想としては、案外少ないというのが正直な気持ちである。

そして、残りの33%の人々と境界を引くことは勇気のいることであり感情ではなく構造に基づいた判断を要する。

本構造を選択し、善意に基づいた行動を無理のない範囲で続けられれば貧困や孤独、絶望を味わうことを少なくできるはずだが、本制度の検証は、一方的な享受に留まる関わり方が選択される場合には、祈りとそれに基づく行動があっても、常に循環するとは限らない、すなわち未来に繋がらないという現実を示した。

ただし、その結果がいい、悪い、ということではなく一つの基準として、制度の持続性を評価できうる、ということである。

これは2026年1月時点の検証に基づく分析結果であり今後、人間の進化と共に、この数字が変化するのかもしれない。

なお、本構造とは別に設定していた社会実践上の目標においても、発展的で持続可能な社会参画率（投票率）として約67%が設定されており、両者が結果的に一致した点は注目に値する。
全体のおよそ2/3に相当する。

本構造において、πは循環が成立している状態を示す構造指標であり、人間社会においては約67%という値が社会の安定性と調和が同時に成立する点を示す一つの数値として観測されたものと結論づける。
